Compliance Reminders

Review all templates against these requirements before client deployment.

TCPA / CTIA — Obtain proper written consent before sending any outbound SMS campaign.

FDCPA — Collections messages must identify the sender as a debt collector and include opt-out language.

State laws — Some states impose stricter time-of-day, frequency, and content rules beyond federal requirements.

HIPAA — Never include protected health information (PHI) in SMS body text.

Character limits — Standard SMS is 160 characters. Exceeding this creates multi-part messages that may incur additional fees.

Human escalation — Every campaign must include a way for the recipient to reach a live person.
